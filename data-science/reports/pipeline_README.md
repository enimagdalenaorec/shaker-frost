# PriPreMa data pipeline: prices, products, vegan status, label nutrition

Builds one table of food products sold in Zagreb with current price, price opportunities, vegan
class with evidence and declared nutrition. Everything is downloaded as files (no live APIs at
query time). Standardisation of product names (ontology) is the next step and is not done here.

(one writer at a time). `data/` is gitignored; scripts and `report/` are committed.

## Outputs (`data/handoff/`)

`data/handoff/` is the folder to send: the parquet files, `HANDOFF.md` (data dictionary: every file, every
`products` column, nutrition sources and gap reasons, how the app uses them; generated by `08_export.py` via
`scripts/handoff_doc.py`) and `reports/` (data_facts, nutrition check, vegan audit, this README). A copy of the
dictionary is in `report/handoff.md`. Main files: `products` (one row per food product), `offers` (product x chain
or shop), `store_prices` (product x store), `price_history` (sampled weeks), `concepts` (search). All join on
`product_key`.

## Decisions

**Prices.** Target days = snapshot minus 0, 7, 14, 21, 28. Per (target, chain) the newest archive on or
before the target day that contains the chain, at most 6 days back (Lidl and Lorenco are missing from
today's archive and come from the day before). Per store the current price is its newest observation.
Effective price = special_price when lower than price. **prilika** = current ≤ 95 % of the median of
the sampled weeks, with ≥ 3 observations; it is not called a sale. **akcija** = the retailer publishes a
special_price. Stale prices are kept and flagged, never dropped: `stale` = the product was not in the
store's last two samples, with `last_seen`, `days_since_seen` and `stale_reason`: 'cjenik poslovnice se
smanjio' (the store's list shrank by more than 20 % after the last sighting, likely a data problem;
Žabac PJ-7 went from ~4,900 to ~3,200 products on 24 Sep) or 'nema ga u novijim cjenicima' (stable list
without it, likely delisted or sold out). The cheapest offer prefers current prices and falls back to
the newest stale one, flagged in `best_stale` / `best_stale_reason`. The archives hold a sample of stores per
chain; stores without a city (dm's national list, Lorenco, two Kaufland entries) are kept as
`scope = 'nacionalno'`. Studenac has no sampled Zagreb store and therefore no Zagreb price.

**Product key.** EAN without leading zeros, so one product across chains and TZH is one row; otherwise
`chain:product_id`. OFF records on GS1 restricted-circulation codes (prefix 2, reused by chains for
different products) are dropped unless the brand agrees (e.g. Lidl 20686321 is Chef Select ricotta
tortelloni, OFF has a Vemondo product under the same code).

**Food filter.** Chain categories decide; uncategorised products (Plodine, Lorenco, 'Ostalo') get a
naive-Bayes label from name words trained on the categorised ones, kept only at ≥ 97 % posterior.

**Vegan classes** (`scripts/06_vegan.py`, word lists in `scripts/vegan_lexicon.py`):

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `vegan` | strong positive evidence and no negative or doubtful signal |
| `potencijalno_vegan` | only medium evidence, or strong evidence with a doubtful ingredient / product type, or alcohol without a vegan label |
| `nesigurno` | no usable evidence, or strong positive and strong negative evidence that contradict |
| `nije_vegan` | animal ingredient, animal word in a name, OFF non-vegan analysis or animal allergen, or an animal category |

Strong positive: OFF vegan label; 'vegan' in a name; OFF ingredient analysis vegan with every ingredient
recognised and our own scan clean; the shop's 'Pogodno za vegane' with an ingredient list our scan
finds clean; a brand whose whole range is plant-based (Alpro, Oatly, Vemondo, Violife, Beyond Meat, ...);
a single-ingredient plant staple (fresh produce, rice, flour, legumes, oils, spices, plain coffee).
Contradictions never become `vegan`: OFF vegan labels were found on 'Baguette peciva sa sirom',
'POP salama', 'Knorr juha kokošja' and gelatine gummies, and bio&bio marks honey sweets as vegan; all
of these land in `nesigurno` or `nije_vegan`. Ingredient scanning removes traces statements ('može
sadržavati'), negations ('bez mlijeka') and plant phrases (kokosovo mlijeko, kakao maslac, mliječna
kiselina, jogurtne kulture) before looking for animal words in hr/en/de/it/sl. Every decision keeps its
evidence as JSON in `vegan_evidence`.

**Shop links.** Shop pages join chain products by EAN; bio&bio has no EAN, so `04c_link_shops.py`
also links by brand (≥ 90), name (≥ 85, brand and size removed) and pack size (± 10 %), best match
only, ties dropped (63 links, all sampled pairs correct). Linked pages are `shop_link = 'ime'`; their
vegan attribute counts as medium evidence and their nutrition ranks after OFF ('trgovina_povezano').

**OFF categories.** A plain plant category (juices, water, produce, legumes, nuts, spices, oils, coffee,
tea, jams) is medium evidence (`potencijalno_vegan` at most). OFF animal categories are not negative
evidence because OFF files plant yogurts under en:yogurts and nori under en:seafood.

**Standardisation.** Two vocabularies in JSON: `vocab/koncepti_A.json` (146 substitutes: plant drinks,
yoghurts, creams, cheeses, meat and fish analogues, egg and gelatine replacers, ...) and `vocab/koncepti_B.json`
(301 plain plant foods), plus `vocab/atributi.json` (68 values in 6 dimensions) and `vocab/kratice.json`.
Each concept: id, standard name, parent (a real tree), family, level, priority, regex patterns and
exclusions over the folded joined names, morpheme roots for LIKE search, synonyms, `zamjenjuje` +
`zamjenjuje_patterni`, positive and negative test names. Assignment is algorithmic: run A first, then B;
main concept = highest priority, deepest level, longest match; other hits are components. The model only
drafted the vocabularies from algorithmic evidence (n-grams, name samples, abbreviations, the draft-4 seed
in `vocab/seed_koncepti_draft4.json` and its false hits); decisions and manual corrections are in
`vocab/odluke_*.json` and `vocab/ispravke_A.json`. Run A got a repair round and a manual review; run B only
had catastrophic errors fixed (one invalid regex dropped, 30 cross-family duplicates merged), 57 minor test
failures remain. Coverage: 91 % of vegan and 59 % of potencijalno products (unlabelled spirits have no concept).
Model cost ~35 USD for standardisation, ~36 USD for the nutrition step (incl. web) (`data/work/llm_calls.jsonl`).

**Nutrition.** Step 7 copies declared values (shop page, OFF), per 100 g / 100 ml, 7 EU values + fibre,
per-field source in `<nutrient>_src`, plausibility flags in `nutrition_note`. Step 11b fills gaps of vegan and
potencijalno_vegan products and never presents an estimate as a label (`<nutrient>_src`, `nutrition_ref`):
`isti_proizvod` (the same product in other packs or shops: same name without pack size and same brand; labels
that agree within 10 % kcal are averaged so every shop shows the same values, the original label stays in
`<nutrient>_etiketa`), then `prosjek_slicnih` (median of the 5 most similar labelled products of the concept,
>= 3 donors, kcal CV <= 0.25) and `ciqual_koncept` (one CIQUAL food per concept + form, chosen by Sonnet from a
code-filtered short list in `vocab/koncept_ciqual.json`); spirits get `ciqual_vrsta_pica` by keyword. On hidden
labels the similar-products median beat CIQUAL in every segment (basic foods 81 vs 77 %, drinks 58 vs 45 %,
processed 65 vs 54 % within 10 % kcal), so it fills first and CIQUAL fills the rest. Numbers and notes in
`report/nutrijenti_provjera.md`. Products still at <= 4 of 7 values were typed by Sonnet (11f): generic foods get `ciqual_naziv`, branded ones a Tavily search read by Sonnet (11c --gaps), supplements, dry tea and non-food stay empty with the reason in `nutrition_note`;
only exact product matches with a URL are applied, source `web` (11e). 11e also completes products that have some of the 7 values: kcal from the label formula or one macro from the kcal
residual (`izracun_etiketa`), saturated fat and sugars as ratios of the product's own fat and carbohydrates, salt and
other macros from similar products scaled to the product's kcal (`omjer_slicnih`); never without a kcal anchor outside
the product's concept. For the app, 11e adds `nutrition_izvor` / `nutrition_izvor_opis` (badge: etiketa_trgovina, etiketa_off, etiketa_web,
etiketa_isti_proizvod, procjena_slicni, procjena_ciqual, procjena_izracun, nema), `nutrition_izvori`, `nutrition_procijenjeno`, and for
every gap `nutrition_nedostaje` (missing fields) with `nutrition_praznina` / `nutrition_praznina_opis` (reason code
and text, e.g. dodatak_prehrani, suhi_caj, alkohol_bez_deklaracije, web_nije_pronaden, etiketa_nepotpuna). Unfilled fields stay NULL with `nutrition_note = 'nije pronađeno'`.

## Known gaps

- About 1,400 vegan / potencijalno_vegan products still have no nutrition (no concept, no donors, CIQUAL refused).
- Coffee and tea mix bases: labels mostly declare the prepared drink, CIQUAL the dry product (accepted).
- Manual decisions: `vocab/neprehrambeno.json` (non-food let through by the filter), bottled spirits get no food
  concept (`scripts/spirits.py`), fruit / vegetable concepts exclude baked goods (`ispravke` in `vocab/koncepti_B.json`).
- Soft drinks, juices, sweets without OFF ingredients stay `nesigurno`; they are rarely recipe items.
- Studenac has no Zagreb store in the archives.
- Vegan precision was measured on a stratified sample reviewed by the model, not by people:
  406/407 (95 % CI 98.6-100 %), see `report/vegan_audit.md`. Recall is not measured.
- 3 TZH pages are 404 and 3 redirect to category pages; a few redirected TZH products may appear twice
  in `shop_products` (old and new id).
